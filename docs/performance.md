## Re-measured after a fix (NFR-REL-02)

| | Sweeper interval | Timeout-path time to available (5 units) | Against 240 s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 5 s | 243.75, 243.83, 243.96, 244.04, 244.09 s (p50 243.96, max 244.09) | over by 3.75 to 4.09 s |
| After | 1 s | 240.85, 240.89, 240.91, 240.93, 240.94 s (p50 240.91, max 240.94; `docs/evidence/m12-rel02-sweep1s.json`) | over by 0.85 to 0.94 s |

Why it happened: a reservation's window is exactly 4 minutes, but the background sweeper that frees it only wakes every few seconds, so the unit is still marked reserved until the next wake. A caller that asks for the unit sees it free at once (the registry frees expired holds before answering); only the stored status lags. The fix cut the lag from about 4.0 s to about 0.9 s, but the stored status cannot be freed before the 240 s mark, so a strict reading of "within 4 min" is still missed by under a second; it can only be met to within one sweep. **Ben decided on 2026-11-15 (in chat): that residual lag counts as met within one sweep, and the decision stands.** Recorded here so the verdict is his, not the assistant's.
